## Week 2

### Mon — Bulk sign-in (second product story)

Same rails, different sentence:

You have a PDF. You have a list of 40 people. You need the fields filled and a signature back.

School trips, volunteer packs, contractor induction, terms of hire. Not a deed. Not a CRM workflow. A folder.

Same pricing as venues. Page: /bulk-sign (and mention it at the bottom of venue conversations when they say “we also send forms to schools”).

### Tue — Founder note (process)

How we write to venues, because people ask:

We send from touch2sign.info on purpose. A cold email should never damage the reputation of a real signing invite. Every note has a human unsubscribe. If you got one and it was a miss, reply and we stop.

### Wed — Mini case (until you have a real one, use a scenario — labelled)

**Scenario, not a customer quote:** 18 children, exclusive hire, 11am Saturday.

Old way: host prints 20 forms, three parents forgot a pen, one form goes home “to sign later.”

New way: link on the booking confirmation. Door sheet shows two unsigned. They scan the walk-in QR. Folder named “Ava — 12 Apr.”

When you have a real pilot, replace this. Do not invent names.

- One post in relevant Facebook groups (not spam-daily).
- One comment on a UK leisure / FEC LinkedIn post about queues or waivers.
- Check PartyFind / Soft Play Finder / SoftPlaylist — if they add vendor listings, use the 25-word blurb.
- Insurers and brokers who sell to trampoline / soft play: one polite note offering a co-branded “how to store consent” PDF (the one-pager). They care about records existing.

## Do not do

- Boosted ads to “parents in London” — wrong buyer.
- Fake review farms.
- “AI-powered waiver platform” language.
- Photos of children without a model-release process you actually have.
