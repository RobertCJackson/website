# Bulk sign-in — second motion

Same product as Venue Disclaimers. Different sentence. Use this when:

- A venue says “we also send forms to schools / hirers”
- The buyer is not a party venue but has a **PDF + a list of people**
- DocuSign / Adobe is too expensive or too heavy for the job

Product page: `/bulk-sign`  
Enquiry: `/contact?use_case=bulk` or hello@touch2sign.info

---

## The sentence

Send one form to fifty people. Get the info and the signature back.

Not a deed. Not a CRM workflow. A folder.

---

## Who

| Segment | Example form | Why they hate email-PDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School trip leads / PTA | Parent consent, medical, photo | 120 replies, 14 missing, one scanned upside down |
| Sports clubs / scouts | Code of conduct, season registration | Volunteers chasing WhatsApp |
| Hire venues | Terms of hire, damage deposit acknowledgement | Every booking, same PDF |
| Facilities / H&S | Contractor induction, site rules | Need a list of who is allowed on site Monday |
| Community / church | Volunteer pack, DBS acknowledgement | Seasonal surge, no admin staff |
| Small HR | Policy acknowledgement, simple offer | Do not need Business-plan Touch2Sign yet |
