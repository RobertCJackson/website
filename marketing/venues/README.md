## What we are selling

Parent consent **before** the party, not at the door.

One event link or QR. Parents sign on mobile. Door checklist. Searchable signed PDFs. From **£10/month for 1,000 forms** (Starter £5 / 250, Scale £25 unlimited-fair-use).

We are **not** ticketing, POS, or a replacement for ROLLER / Venue-OS / XEPOS. The solicitor owns the wording. We own signing, storage, and the audit trail.

Same product, second story: send one simple form to a list (school trip, volunteer pack, contractor induction) and get info + signature back.

## Who to talk to (ICP)

**Primary — small party venues, owner-operated**

- Soft play centres and play cafés with birthday packages / exclusive hire
- Independent trampoline / jump / clip-and-climb sites (franchisees, not HQ)
- Farm attractions and seasonal play barns that host parties
- Party rooms and hire-only toddler venues

**Buyer:** owner, general manager, or the person who actually prints the clipboards on Saturday.

**Pain you can name in one sentence:** fifteen WhatsApp threads and a binder at the door, then you cannot find the form when something happens.

**Disqualify**

- Already on an all-in-one venue OS where waivers are bundled and they are happy
- Corporate leisure groups (GLL, Namco, large Gravity HQ) unless a local GM asks
- Anyone who wants *us* to write the legal waiver

**Secondary — bulk sign-in**

- School trip coordinators, sports clubs, scout groups, churches, volunteer orgs
- Facilities / H&S sending the same induction PDF to contractors
- Hire venues sending terms of hire to every booking

## How we talk

Match the [touch2sign.info](https://touch2sign.info/) voice: slightly apologetic, specific, no fake urgency. Outreach goes from `.info` on purpose so a cold note never hurts a real signing invite.

Do:

- Name their venue type. Mention parties, the door, the binder.
- Price in the first email (£10 / 1,000). It is the whole point.
- Offer 15 minutes, not a “discovery call.”
- One-click unsubscribe, and mean it.

Do not:

- Claim we replace ROLLER
- Claim our templates are legally sufficient in every jurisdiction
- Inflate social proof we do not have yet (“trusted by 500 venues”)
- Attach a 12-page PDF. Link `/venues` or the `.info` page.

## First two weeks (do this, in order)

1. Import `prospects.csv` into your mail tool. Status starts as `new`. Verify each address on the venue site before send — listings rot.
2. Send **email 1** from `hello@touch2sign.info`, 20–30 independent party venues, not Clip ’n Climb HQ.
3. Post two LinkedIn notes and one Facebook venue-owner group post from `social-noise.md`.
4. Reply to anyone who says “we use paper” with the 15-minute walkthrough. First pilots get hands-on setup.
5. For “we already have waivers in our booking system” — park them. Do not discount into an all-in-one fight.
6. Keep a simple log: sent / replied / unsubscribed / pilot / not now.
