## Reply snippets

**“We already use ROLLER / Venue-OS.”**  
Keep it. We are not trying to replace bookings or the till. If waivers are already bundled and staff actually use them, you probably do not need us. If parents still sign a paper sheet because the waiver module is clunky, that is the gap we fill.

**“Can you write our disclaimer?”**  
No — and you should be glad. Your solicitor owns the wording. We take that PDF (or text), put fields on it, and store the signed copies with an audit trail.

**“Is this legally binding for kids?”**  
Parent consent / acknowledgement of risk is what venues collect. Whether a waiver would stand up in court is a legal question for your advisor and your insurer, not a software claim. What we *can* do is prove who signed what, when, on which device.

**“We’re tiny / seasonal.”**  
Starter is £5/month for 250 forms. Seasonal sites often pause or go annual (2 months free).

**“Send pricing.”**  
Starter £5 / 250 forms. Standard £10 / 1,000 (most venues). Scale £25, fair-use ~10,000, up to 5 locations. Extra 1,000 forms = £10.

**Angry / “how did you get this email?”**  
Public address on your website / contact page. Sorry for the noise. Unsubscribed. Won’t write again.
