## 6. Support (when you have paying venues asking the same three questions)

**Title:** Venue + legal support  
**Tools:** Gmail, HubSpot tickets, Help Centre doc in Notion  
**Description seed:** Draft replies to “can you write our waiver?”, “does this replace ROLLER?”, “is it legally binding for kids?” using the snippets in marketing/venues/outreach-sequence.md. Never send. Never give legal advice. Escalate anything that smells like an incident/insurance claim.

**First message when you create it:**

```
Read marketing/venues/outreach-sequence.md reply snippets and the legal line in the README.
Build a 10-row FAQ in Notion/Drive. Draft-only support. Wait for paying customers before a routine.
```
